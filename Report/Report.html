Section 01

Business Problem

Why cancellation analysis is mission-critical for hotel revenue management

Hotel booking cancellations can negatively impact occupancy, revenue forecasting, and operational planning. The objective of this analysis is to identify cancellation patterns and uncover the factors most strongly associated with booking cancellations.

Revenue Impact

Cancelled bookings represent direct lost revenue, particularly severe when high-ADR reservations are affected — as they generate the greatest per-night income.

Operational Impact

Unpredictable cancellations disrupt staffing schedules, room preparation, and supply chain management for food and amenities.

Forecasting Impact

High cancellation volatility undermines the accuracy of occupancy forecasts, making strategic capacity planning significantly more difficult.

Section 04

ADR Analysis by Hotel Type

Examining average daily rate performance across hotel categories over time

The Average Daily Rate (ADR) analysis showed that Resort Hotels generally maintain higher room rates than City Hotels. Although City Hotels occasionally recorded higher ADR values on certain dates, the highest pricing peaks were consistently observed in Resort Hotels.

This indicates stronger pricing power and revenue potential within the Resort Hotel segment.

Figure 03
Average Daily Rate — City Hotel vs Resort Hotel Over Time
Time Series
Figure 03 — Resort Hotels consistently demonstrate higher ADR peaks throughout the observation period (2014–2017), confirming superior pricing power relative to City Hotels.
Key Observation

The highest pricing peaks are consistently observed in Resort Hotels, while City Hotels maintain a more moderate and steady rate profile. This pattern suggests that Resort Hotels benefit from leisure demand inelasticity — guests are less price-sensitive when booking vacation experiences.

Monthly Reservation Trends

Seasonal patterns in booking volumes and cancellation concentrations throughout the year

Booking activity showed clear seasonal patterns throughout the year. August recorded the highest number of successful reservations, indicating strong customer demand. In contrast, January recorded the highest number of cancellations, while December showed the lowest number of successful bookings.

These trends highlight the importance of seasonal planning and demand forecasting.

Section 08

Market Segment Analysis

Evaluating booking volume and cancellation risk across customer acquisition channels

Booking Volume by Segment

Online Travel Agencies (Online TA) generated the largest share of reservations, accounting for approximately 47% of all bookings. Offline Travel Agencies, Groups, Direct bookings, and Corporate bookings contributed smaller portions.

The results indicate a strong dependence on online booking platforms for customer acquisition.

Cancellation Volume by Segment

Online Travel Agencies also generated the highest share of cancellations, contributing approximately 46% of all cancelled bookings. Group reservations accounted for 27% of cancellations, followed by Offline Travel Agencies at 18%.

These segments represent the largest sources of cancellation risk within the dataset.

Section 09

ADR & Cancellation Relationship

Exploring the correlation between room pricing and booking commitment

A comparison between cancelled and non-cancelled reservations revealed that cancelled bookings generally had higher ADR values. This suggests that higher-priced reservations are more likely to be cancelled than lower-priced bookings.

Since these bookings generate higher revenue, their cancellation can have a greater financial impact on hotel performance.

Figure 08
Average Daily Rate — Cancelled vs Not Cancelled Reservations
Time Series Comparison
Figure 08 — The consistently elevated ADR trajectory of cancelled bookings (dashed amber) compared to completed reservations (solid teal) confirms a positive relationship between price point and cancellation likelihood.
Critical Business Finding

The inverse relationship between ADR and booking commitment creates a compounding revenue risk: the bookings generating the highest per-night revenue are simultaneously the most likely to be cancelled. This demands a targeted approach to securing high-value reservations through advance payment policies, loyalty incentives, and personalized retention outreach.

Section 10

Key Insights

The seven principal findings distilled from across the full analysis

1

City Hotels — The High-Risk Category

City Hotels experience significantly higher cancellation rates than Resort Hotels, making them the primary target for cancellation reduction initiatives.

2

Resort Hotels — Superior Pricing Power

Resort Hotels maintain higher ADR values and stronger pricing performance throughout the year, indicating greater revenue potential and booking stability.

3

August — The Peak Demand Window

August recorded the highest successful booking volume, representing the most critical period for revenue maximization and capacity optimization.

4

January — The Cancellation Peak

January recorded the highest cancellation volume, suggesting post-holiday financial pressure or plan reconsideration as a dominant behavioral driver.

5

Portugal — A Geographic Concentration Risk

Portugal accounted for approximately 70% of all cancellations — an extraordinary market concentration requiring dedicated investigation and strategy.

6

Online TA — The Double-Edged Channel

Online Travel Agencies generated both the highest booking volume and the highest cancellation volume, illustrating the inherent tension in OTA-dependent acquisition strategies.

7

High-ADR Bookings — Elevated Cancellation Risk

Higher ADR bookings showed a greater tendency to be cancelled, creating a dangerous correlation where the most financially valuable reservations carry the highest cancellation risk. This amplifies the revenue impact of every cancellation in the premium pricing segment.

Section 11

Strategic Recommendations

Six actionable interventions to reduce cancellations and protect hotel revenue

01
High Priority

Prioritize Cancellation Reduction Strategies for City Hotels

Prioritize cancellation reduction strategies for City Hotels. With a ~41% cancellation rate significantly exceeding Resort Hotels, City Hotel operations require dedicated intervention — including stricter cancellation policies, advance deposit requirements, and enhanced guest communication protocols.

02
Urgent Investigation

Investigate the Unusually High Cancellation Rate from Portugal

Investigate the unusually high cancellation rate from Portugal. The 70% concentration demands a dedicated market analysis to identify whether systemic booking behavior, policy weaknesses, or external economic factors are the primary drivers — followed by market-specific corrective measures.

03
Channel Strategy

Strengthen Direct Booking Channels

Strengthen direct booking channels to reduce dependency on third-party platforms. Investing in direct booking incentives — loyalty programs, exclusive rate offers, and enhanced website conversion — can shift volume to channels with demonstrably lower cancellation rates.

04
Channel Policy

Review OTA and Group Booking Cancellation Policies

Review cancellation policies associated with Online Travel Agencies and Group bookings. As the two highest-risk segments generating 46% and 27% of all cancellations respectively, renegotiating OTA contract terms and tightening group deposit structures can meaningfully reduce overall cancellation rates.

05
Revenue Protection

Monitor High-ADR Reservations More Closely

Monitor high-ADR reservations more closely, as they carry a higher cancellation risk. Implementing proactive retention touchpoints — pre-arrival engagement, flexible upgrade offers, and personalized communication — can convert high-value at-risk bookings into secured revenue.

06
Revenue Management

Leverage Seasonal Booking and Pricing Trends

Use seasonal booking and pricing trends to optimize revenue management and occupancy planning. The clear seasonal patterns identified — with August peaks and January troughs — provide actionable input for dynamic pricing strategies, promotional campaign timing, and staffing optimization frameworks.

Section 12

Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that hotel booking cancellations are influenced by hotel type, booking channel, customer location, seasonality, and pricing behavior. City Hotels, Online Travel Agencies, and high-ADR reservations emerged as the most significant risk areas. By addressing these factors through targeted pricing, channel management, and cancellation reduction strategies, hotels can improve occupancy rates, reduce revenue loss, and enhance overall business performance.
