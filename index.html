Project Overview

The SQL Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis project investigates the factors influencing reservation cancellations using SQL for data extraction and transformation, Python for visualization, and professional business reporting for executive communication. The analysis examines hotel types, booking channels, pricing behavior, seasonal demand, customer geography, and reservation outcomes to identify operational risks and revenue optimization opportunities.

Project Highlights

🏨 Reservation Status Analysis

Analyzed 118,897 hotel reservations to evaluate successful bookings and cancellations. The project identified cancellation behavior across multiple business dimensions, helping reveal operational risks and opportunities for improving occupancy management.

📊 Hotel Performance Comparison

Compared City Hotels and Resort Hotels to understand differences in cancellation rates, pricing performance, and booking behavior. City Hotels recorded substantially higher cancellation rates, highlighting the need for targeted cancellation reduction strategies.

💰 ADR & Revenue Analysis

Explored Average Daily Rate (ADR) trends across hotel types and throughout the year. The analysis demonstrated that Resort Hotels consistently maintained stronger pricing power, while higher-priced reservations were also more likely to be cancelled.

📅 Seasonal Booking Trends

Investigated monthly booking patterns, identifying August as the strongest month for completed reservations and January as the highest-risk month for cancellations. These insights support demand forecasting and seasonal revenue planning.

🌍 Geographic Cancellation Insights

Examined customer countries contributing to cancelled reservations. Portugal represented approximately 70% of all cancellations, revealing a significant regional concentration that deserves further business investigation.

📈 Market Segment Analysis

Evaluated booking channels including Online Travel Agencies, Direct bookings, Groups, Corporate customers, and Offline Travel Agencies. Online Travel Agencies generated both the largest booking volume and the largest share of cancellations.

Business Insights

📉 Higher Cancellation Risk

City Hotels and Online Travel Agencies were identified as the primary contributors to reservation cancellations, indicating opportunities to improve cancellation policies and booking management strategies.

💲 Pricing Behaviour

Higher ADR reservations demonstrated a greater likelihood of cancellation, suggesting that premium-priced bookings require closer monitoring and targeted retention efforts.

📆 Seasonal Planning

Demand fluctuated considerably throughout the year, emphasizing the importance of seasonal forecasting, staffing optimization, and revenue management during peak and low-demand periods.

🎯 Strategic Recommendations

The analysis provides actionable recommendations including strengthening direct booking channels, reviewing OTA cancellation policies, optimizing pricing strategies, and improving occupancy forecasting.
